~~~ About Us ~~~




Coaching Classes was established on 12 April 2010 with enormous vision to provide the best education in a different, innovative and pleasant learning environment in the challenging field of Pre-Engineering entrance exams.


Coaching Classes is committed to help the students in career vision. We inculcate the values of learning. Coaching Classes' target is to make a difference in the way students think and approach problems. It provides excellent study inputs to the students with elective study strategy and dedicated teaching staff in preparing students for these highly competitive examinations.


The devotion and expertise of our teachers and the methodologies followed by us have generated a great confidence among the students and parents, due to which Coaching Classes is regarded as a premier institute for JEE (Main+Advanced), NEET-UG, Olympiads, BITSAT, MHT-CET & other Pre-Engineering competitive exams preparation at Nashik.


With PRACTICE, PERSISTENCE & PERFORMANCE under the meticulous mentorship of our teachers, Coaching Classes has been delivering outstanding and quality results each year from Nashik.


An experience that is one of its kind and has become mightier with each passing year.
